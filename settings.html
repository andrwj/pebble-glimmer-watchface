Display & Performance
Show Second Hand
When enabled, the second hand runs every second. When disabled, the watch updates once per minute to save battery.
Show Second Hand After Startup
Number of seconds to display the second hand after rolling on startup before switching to minute updates. (0 to 60s)
10s
Show Second Hand On Reappear
When the watchface reappears after being obscured by notifications or other windows, show the second hand again for the configured duration.
Sleep & Wake
Enable Sleep Mode
Automatically enter deep sleep mode at the configured time to maximize battery life.
Sleep Time (HH:MM)
Start time to enter deep sleep mode. Hands turn dark gray and all ticks stop.
Wake Time (HH:MM)
End time of sleep mode. Starting the watch outside this range resumes normal display.
Wake Briefly on Reappear
When reappearing from notifications during sleep hours, briefly wake up to show the updated time via rolling animation before returning to sleep.
※ Note: During sleep hours, launching the watch will display the updated time and then return to sleep. Outside this range, normal operation resumes.
Tap to Update Time
Enable Tap to Update
Tap the watch to roll hands and update the current time.
Tap Count
Number of consecutive taps required to trigger time update (1 or 2). Setting to 2 prevents accidental triggering when moved.
Recognize Tap While Sleeping
Allow tap recognition even during deep sleep mode to briefly wake up and check the time.
※ Note: When tapped during sleep, the hands roll to show the current time, stay visible for the configured duration, and then return to sleep.
